Quist Quest

Tribes · Islands · Ambition

A turn-based strategy game for your phone. Lead a tribe across a low-poly 3D hex world: explore, grow cities, research, trade, make peace or take what you need.

Coming soon to iPhone and Android

A world in your hand

Captured from the game running at phone size.

Two ways to play

Quest

30 turns. The top score wins — or finish it early by conquest, or by holding two-thirds of every settlement on the map.

Conquest

No turn limit. Win by conquest, or as an alliance of every survivor. On roomy worlds you can also win by keeping a Beacon burning.

What's in the game

  • Rivals that play fair

    AI rivals follow the same rules and the same fog of war you do. Hard means they play smarter — focus-firing, scheming, racing you — never cheating.

    Three difficulties: Easy, Normal, Hard.

  • Worlds that feel crowded

    Six world sizes and three shapes: continents, one big landmass, or islands. Up to 16 rivals.

    By default the map is tight, so you meet someone in the first few turns.

  • 16 tribes

    Each has its own perk and a unique unit — forest runners, ice walkers, cliff dwellers, canyon slingers, castle knights.

    Four are included. Twelve are premium at $1.99 each, a one-time purchase. Premium tribes play differently, not stronger.

  • Research and build

    24 technologies in six branches. Cities level up and pick a reward each level.

    Buildings, ports, boats and warships.

  • Diplomacy that remembers

    Peace, alliances, embassies, trade routes and technology swaps.

    Betrayals are remembered.

  • Learn in three minutes

    A guided tutorial plus two lessons teach the game by playing it.

    No account needed. Games and settings are saved on your device.

Pick your people

Every tribe starts with a different technology and fields a unit nobody else can build. The tribe you choose changes how your first ten turns go.

  • Thornwood

    Forest hunters. Cross woods at full speed and fight from cover. A good first tribe.

  • Saltmere

    Coastal traders. Every harvest costs one ember less, and the sea is theirs.

  • Highcrag

    Mountain folk. Climb peaks from turn one and see further from the high ground.

  • Emberhold

    Fire-worshipping riders with a richer capital and a siege engine that throws flame.

Twelve more — shadow scholars, cliff dwellers, northern mammoth riders, canyon slingers, castle knights, deep-digging smiths, jungle darters, desert nomads, bear clans, sun priests, tinkerers and lake mystics — are available as one-time purchases.
