Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Quist Quest is made by Nate Lindquist, an independent developer in the Pacific Northwest. This policy explains what the game and this website do with information. It is written to describe what the software actually does today, not what it might do later.

The short version

The game keeps your saved games and settings on your own device. It does not create an account, does not ask for your name or email, and does not send your play data anywhere. This website has no cookies, no analytics and no advertising.

What the game stores, and where

Quist Quest saves the following on your device only, using your device's local storage:

  • Your game in progress, so you can put the phone down and come back to it.
  • Your settings, including difficulty, sound and the colour-blind option.
  • Your records: best scores, the boards you have played and your rating.
  • Which premium tribes you have unlocked.
  • Your progress through the tutorial and lessons.

This information stays on the device. We have no servers, so there is nowhere for it to be sent. If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it.

What the game sends

The game does not transmit your play data. There is no account system, no login, no cloud save and no analytics in the game.

Purchases

Twelve of the sixteen tribes are one-time purchases. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see or receive your payment details. The app is told only whether a purchase succeeded, and records the unlocked tribe on your device.

Apple and Google handle their own data under their own privacy policies.

Children

Quist Quest does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Because the game collects nothing, there is nothing for us to hold, sell or disclose.

This website

This site is a set of plain pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, shows no advertising and uses no trackers or email-signup services.

Two things are worth stating plainly:

  • Web fonts. The pages load their typefaces from Google Fonts. Doing so means your browser requests those font files from Google, which involves your IP address reaching Google's servers. No cookies are set by this.
  • Hosting. GitHub serves these pages and, like any web host, processes the requests it receives, including IP addresses, for security and reliability. That processing is covered by GitHub's own privacy statement.

Your choices

Because the game holds nothing about you off your device, there is no account to close and no data for us to export or delete. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will be described here rather than made quietly.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@quistquest.com
