Support

Quist Quest is made by one person. Email gets read, and a real answer comes back.

Email: support@quistquest.com

Common questions

Where are my saved games kept?

On your phone. Quist Quest saves your game in progress, your settings and your records on the device itself. There is no account and nothing is stored online.

That also means deleting the app deletes your saves, and they will not come back when you reinstall. If you are clearing space and want to keep a game, finish it first.

I bought tribes and they're gone. How do I get them back?

Open the Tribes screen and tap Restore purchases. The App Store or Google Play confirms what you own and the tribes unlock again.

Use the same Apple or Google account you bought them with. Purchases follow the store account, not the device, so a new phone is fine. If a restore does not bring them back, email us with the store account's region and the approximate purchase date.

What are premium tribes?

There are 16 tribes. Four are included with the game. The other twelve are $1.99 each as a one-time purchase, unlocked forever on your store account.

Premium tribes are designed to play differently, not to be stronger. Each one starts with a different technology, has its own perk and fields a unique unit. A premium tribe does not win fights a starting tribe would lose; it changes which opening and which map suit you.

Something's broken. How do I report it?

Email support@quistquest.com and include:

  • Your device (for example, iPhone 14, Pixel 8).
  • Your operating system version (for example, iOS 18.2, Android 15).
  • What happened, and what you expected instead.
  • What you were doing right before it happened — the more exact, the faster it gets fixed.
  • A screenshot or screen recording if you have one.

If the game showed a seed code, include that too. It lets the same world be rebuilt exactly, which usually turns a vague bug into a fixed one.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account, ever. The game is single-player against AI rivals and runs without a connection, apart from making a purchase, which goes through the App Store or Google Play.

Can I ask for a feature?

Yes. Email it. Quist Quest is deliberately kept simple, so not everything gets added, but suggestions do get read and some of them ship.
